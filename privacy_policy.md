# Privacy Policy for Word Search

**Last Updated:** November 4, 2026

Welcome to **Word Search** ("the App"), developed and published by **PlaySphere** ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparent data practices. 

This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you download, install, and play Word Search.

---

## 1. Overview & Summary

- **No Personal Accounts Required:** You do not need to register, create an account, log in, or provide your real name, email, or phone number to play the game.
- **Local Gameplay Storage:** Your puzzle progress, high scores, collected coins, unlocked categories, and audio preferences are stored **exclusively on your local device**.
- **No Sensitive Device Access:** The App does not request or access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage media, or precise GPS location.
- **In-Game Advertisements:** We use **Google AdMob** to display in-game ads, which may process anonymous device identifiers and interaction statistics to serve and measure advertisements.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The App stores game state data locally on your device using Android SQLite/Room database storage:
- Solved levels and unlocked puzzle categories
- In-game currency (coins) and remaining hints
- Gameplay statistics (completion times, win streaks)
- Sound effects and background music volume preferences

*This data remains on your local device and is never uploaded, shared, or transmitted to any external server.*

### B. Information Automatically Processed by Third Parties
We do not collect personal data directly. However, third-party software development kits (SDKs) integrated into the App automatically process certain technical, non-personally identifiable information:

#### Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) & Google Play Services
We integrate Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial ads. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
- **Device Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (AAID / `AD_ID`) and resettable diagnostic identifiers.
- **Device & Hardware Information:** Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, network carrier, system language, and screen dimensions.
- **Ad Interaction Events:** Ad impressions, views, clicks, and completion states.
- **App Performance & Diagnostics:** Crash logs, application responsiveness data, and API latency measurements used for service stability and invalid click/fraud prevention.

---

## 3. How We Use the Information

The information processed via our third-party advertising partners is used strictly for:
- Delivering and formatting banner and interstitial advertisements during gameplay.
- Measuring ad delivery performance and verifying impressions.
- Protecting against fraud, security risks, bot traffic, and policy violations.
- Monitoring app stability and resolving technical crashes across different Android devices.

## 6. Your Rights & How to Control Advertisements

You can manage or opt out of personalized advertisements at any time directly through your Android device settings:

### Steps to Reset or Delete Your Advertising ID:
1. Open your Android device **Settings**.
2. Navigate to **Google** > **All services** (or **Privacy**) > **Ads**.
3. Select **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**.

*Resetting or deleting your Advertising ID stops ad networks from using cross-app data to build an advertising profile for personalized ads. You will continue to see non-personalized, contextual ads.*
